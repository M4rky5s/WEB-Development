How to Make Pizza Step-By-Step

  1. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat and lightly oil grate.
  2. Make dough: Pour warm water into a large bowl; dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let stand
    until yeast softens and begins to form a creamy foam, about 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. Mix in flour, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and salt until dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
  4. Turn onto a lightly floured surface. Knead until smooth, about 8 minutes.
  5. Place dough in a well-oiled bowl and cover with a damp cloth.
  6. Set aside to rise until doubled, about 1 hour. Punch down; knead in garlic and basil. Set aside to
    rise for 1 more hour, or until doubled again.
  7. Meanwhile, make garlic oil: Combine 1/2 cup olive oil with minced garlic in a microwave-safe cup or
    bowl. Heat for 30 seconds in the microwave.
  8. Make pizzas: Punch down dough and divide in half. Form each half into an oblong shape 3/8 to 1/2 inch thick.
  9. Carefully place one piece of dough on the hot grill. Dough will begin to puff almost immediately.
    When the bottom crust has lightly browned, turn dough over using two spatulas.
  10. Working quickly, brush garlic oil over crust.
  11. Top with 1/2 of each of the following: tomato sauce, chopped tomatoes, olives, red peppers, cheese, and basil.
  12. Close the lid and cook until cheese melts. Remove from grill and set aside to cool for a few minutes.
    Repeat with second piece of dough.
  13. Serve hot and enjoy!
