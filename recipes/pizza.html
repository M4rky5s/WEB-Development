Description

If you're looking for a homemade pizza crust recipe that's great for beginners, you're in luck. This top-rated recipe is super easy to throw together on a
whim – and it puts the store-bought stuff to shame. Learn how to make the best pizza crust of your life with just a few ingredients, find out how to shape the dough,
and get our best storage secrets.

Ingredients

The Allrecipes community adores this pizza recipe because it's incredibly customizable, so you can easily alter the ingredient list to suit your needs.
If you want to stay true to the original recipe, though, these are the ingredients you'll need to add to your grocery list:

  • Tomato sauce
  • Chopped Tomatoes
  • Sliced Black Olives
  • Roasted Red Peppers
  • Shredded Mozzarella Cheese

How to Make Pizza Step-By-Step

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Make dough: Pour warm water into a large bowl; dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let stand
    until yeast softens and begins to form a creamy foam, about 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. Mix in flour, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and salt until dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
  4. Turn onto a lightly floured surface. Knead until smooth, about 8 minutes.
  5. Place dough in a well-oiled bowl and cover with a damp cloth.
  6. Set aside to rise until doubled, about 1 hour. Punch down; knead in garlic and basil. Set aside to
    rise for 1 more hour, or until doubled again.
  7. Meanwhile, make garlic oil: Combine 1/2 cup olive oil with minced garlic in a microwave-safe cup or
    bowl. Heat for 30 seconds in the microwave.
  8. Preheat an outdoor grill for high heat; brush the grate with garlic oil.
  9. Make pizzas: Punch down dough and divide in half. Form each half into an oblong shape 3/8 to 1/2 inch thick.
  10. Carefully place one piece of dough on the hot grill. Dough will begin to puff almost immediately.
    When the bottom crust has lightly browned, turn dough over using two spatulas.
  11. Working quickly, brush garlic oil over crust.
  12. Top with 1/2 of each of the following: tomato sauce, chopped tomatoes, olives, red peppers, cheese, and basil.
  13. Close the lid and cook until cheese melts. Remove from grill and set aside to cool for a few minutes.
    Repeat with second piece of dough.
  14. Serve hot and enjoy!
